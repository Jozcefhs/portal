# Hospital appointments and clinic availability

Development increment: 28 October 2026. Web companion and shared backend on `codex/hospital-edition`. Test and push only; no deployment, compilation or installer release. Verification uses synthetic records, without accessing production patients.

## Staff workflow

Appointments uses the existing **Reception and visits** module and patient registration dependency. The Hospital workspace has an **Appointments** tab. Opening the workspace or tab does not load schedules, appointments or history.

1. A Hospital Administrator, Super Admin or Medical Director opens **Doctor availability**, chooses **New clinic schedule**, and records the doctor's staff username, date, receiving department and WAT availability windows. Doctors can configure their own schedules. Each branch/doctor/date has one stable schedule; separate windows accommodate breaks. Windows cannot overlap. Changes retain previous versions. An administrator must reschedule or cancel affected appointments before removing their time windows or deactivating that day.
2. Reception/Front Desk, administrators, Doctors and Medical Directors can book an appointment from an existing patient registration. Find the patient by the existing surname, hospital-number or NFC-card lookup and choose **Book appointment**. No new patient identity is created.
3. Select dates and **Load clinic schedules**, choose **Use this clinic**, then enter a permanent booking reference, start/end times and patient confirmation. The server checks the current patient, clinician, branch and availability. Appointments are future times on one WAT date, up to twelve hours long and within the next year. There are at most twelve availability windows and 96 bookings per clinic/resource/day. Times are entered to the minute.
4. The patient and doctor cannot have overlapping appointments across Hospital branches or overlapping theatre reservations. These checks share the existing resource/day guard documents with theatre. Conflict messages do not reveal another patient's identity or branch. These are planned-time checks; arrival does not assert that a clinician is free from an overrunning consultation or procedure.
5. Choose dates in **Appointment register** to view bookings. **Open appointment** fetches its current state and patient identity. A scheduled booking can be rescheduled with a reason. Its permanent reference, patient and original follow-up visit link stay fixed; the previous booking remains in its history. Rescheduling releases the old times atomically with reserving the new times.
6. On the scheduled WAT date, an officer with appointment and reception-write permission confirms arrival. Check-in creates one reception visit linked to the booking. If the patient already has an open visit, refresh the appointment and explicitly confirm linking the displayed visit. Existing clinical, admission, billing and department fields remain intact. A visit already linked to another appointment is not reassigned. Clinical roles without reception-write permission cannot check patients in.
7. **Follow-up appointment** on a Reception visit opens booking for the same patient and retains the original visit reference. That visit may be open or closed. The server verifies the same patient/branch; no clinical notes are copied into the appointment.
8. Cancel an unarrived booking with a reason, or record **Not attended** after its scheduled end. Both preserve the booking and release its reserved times. Arrived appointments remain linked to their original visit; use the existing visit/clinical workflow for subsequent care. Cancellation does not erase payments, waive charges or delete clinical records.
9. The dated **Appointment history** displays booking, rescheduling, cancellation, non-attendance and arrival with the officer, time, saved booking and reason. Earlier event snapshots are immutable. Nurses and Medical Records Officers may read appointments when assigned the reception module; cashiers do not gain this access through finance permissions.

## Integrity, privacy and database use

- Every mutation uses document preconditions and one atomic commit covering current state, history, audit, request receipt and applicable schedule/resource/visit guards. Competing staff operations fail without partial reservations or duplicate visits.
- Stable branch/reference appointment IDs prevent fresh repeated clicks from creating another booking. Shared patient/doctor reservations also reject overlapping duplicates with different references. Receipt replays verify the original payload, including after a lost response or after the appointment date has passed. Changed payloads cannot reuse a receipt.
- Appointment arrival and walk-in check-in use the same open-visit guard. Linking an existing visit also checks its current revision, so closure and concurrent clinical changes cannot be overwritten.
- Booking, rescheduling and check-in do not create charges, receipts, stock movements or clinical entries. Existing NFC patient identities and record relationships are retained.
- Hospital edition, subscription, role, module and branch checks apply on the server. Doctor availability lists expose windows and a booked count, without patient/booking identifiers from the reservation maps. Appointment details expose a small reception visit summary, excluding confidential clinical content. Audit identity and outcome are server-owned; request date filters cannot overwrite them.
- Registers require dates of at most 93 days and fetch 31/display 30 records with timestamp/document-ID cursors. There are no whole-history overlap queries or per-row patient lookups. New index definitions are prepared in `firestore.hospital.indexes.json`; they are not deployed in this pass.
- Loaded pages are reused in a bounded 40-page memory cache. Refresh fetches updated data; mutations invalidate appointment views. Significant actions read current documents and revalidate on the server. No polling or browser-storage persistence of patient data is added. Branch/session changes clear private records and forms; unsaved forms require a discard decision, including after a rejected save.
- Measured synthetic operation budgets: booking uses six exact document reads, one staff query limited to two matches and seven atomic writes; new-visit arrival uses four exact reads and six writes. A completed mutation retry needs one receipt read and zero writes. A register page uses one bounded query and one access-audit write, with zero patient reads. An arrived appointment uses three detail reads, following its saved visit without another open-visit lookup. Unchanged schedule/booking saves write nothing. Authentication, branch configuration, explicit context reloads and conflict retries are separate from these application budgets; these are not measured cloud billing totals.
- Backup discovery includes `AppointmentSchedules`, `AppointmentScheduleEvents`, `Appointments`, `AppointmentEvents` and the shared resource/visit collections. No existing records are migrated or deleted.

## Appointment waitlist — 14 November 2026

Open **Appointments → Appointment waitlist**. The list is branch-specific. Choose **Waiting** and **Load waitlist** for pending requests, including earlier preferred dates; use **Booked** or **Cancelled** with a date range for completed history. Each page contains at most 30 records. Repeating a loaded request or returning to this task reuses its cached page; Refresh fetches current records.

To add a request, find an existing patient under **Patients and NFC cards**, choose **Book appointment → Add to waitlist**. Enter a permanent reference, receiving department matching the clinic schedule, preferred dates, optional doctor's username and scheduling note. Confirm the patient's identity. An active patient may have one waiting request per department in this branch. A different department can have its own request.

Open a request to change its preferences with a reason, or cancel it with a reason. The original identity/reference/department and every snapshot remain. Cancelled/booked records cannot be silently revived. A new reference can be used for a later request. Cancel an existing booked appointment through its appointment workflow; that does not automatically reopen its old waiting request.

**Book this waiting request** retains its patient and reference. Choose a matching clinic and a time within the preferred dates. Broad preferences prefill only the first 31 days for schedule lookup; change the lookup dates to inspect a later part of the preference. Existing active clinician, patient, branch, availability and clinic/theatre overlap checks apply. Saving atomically creates the appointment, reserves capacity, marks the request Booked, releases its active-waiting guard, and records history/audit. A simultaneous cancellation/edit or competing booking cannot leave a partial conversion. Retries cannot create another appointment.

**Preview / print request**, **Preview / print loaded page** and **Preview / print loaded history** use the saved/loaded snapshots with no additional requests. Unsaved edits are not printed as saved facts. History requires at most a 93-day range. Records are cleared on branch/session change. Reception/Front Desk, Doctors, Medical Directors and authorised administrators use existing appointment permissions; read-only roles cannot create, edit, cancel or convert requests.

No messages, clinical entries, visits, invoices, payments or stock movements are created by this workflow. Creating a request uses four exact document reads and five atomic writes; editing preferences avoids rewriting its unchanged guard. Conversion uses eight exact reads, one clinician query limited to two matches and ten atomic writes. These budgets exclude authentication, branch configuration, intentional detail reloads and concurrency retries. Completed receipt replay uses one read and zero writes; unchanged saves write nothing. Each list page uses one bounded query and one access-audit write, without per-patient lookups. New collections are included in Hospital backup discovery, with three ascending branch/status/date indexes.

Verification: `tests/hospital-appointment-waitlist.test.mjs` (18 cases), `scripts/verify-hospital-waitlist-ui.mjs`, the existing appointment browser workflow and the full top-level regression suite (4,764 passing tests). Synthetic browser checks cover creation, preferences, history, printing, booking, cancellation, date validation, caching, branch/session clearing, access controls and mobile overflow. Live deployment evidence is kept in `hospital-owner-demo.md`.

## Recurring clinic schedules — 14 November 2026

Open **Appointments → Recurring clinic schedules**. A Hospital Administrator, Super Admin or Medical Director can configure branch templates; Doctors can configure their own. Read-only appointment roles can inspect saved templates and dated history. The module opens without requesting any templates, schedules, patients or history.

1. Choose **New recurring clinic**. Enter a permanent reference, descriptive clinic name, doctor's staff username, receiving department, weekdays and one or more WAT time windows. Save the template. Multiple templates may cover different weekday patterns; all of them use the same branch/doctor/day schedule identity.
2. Choose dates, at most 31 calendar days within the next year, and **Preview clinic dates**. Only selected weekdays are checked. The preview identifies missing days to create, matching days already available and conflicting/inactive days needing review. It writes nothing and shows no patient identifiers.
3. Confirm **Create missing clinic days**. The server recomputes the preview and validates the clinician. It creates only missing days and their histories. Existing manual schedules, inactive exceptions and all bookings are kept. If a concurrent change invalidates the preview, no partial generation is posted; refresh the template if needed and preview again. Lost responses can retry the same action safely.
4. Use **Doctor availability** to load the created days and book appointments normally. Existing patient/clinician/theatre overlap and clinic capacity safeguards apply. A conflicting date must be reviewed and changed through its ordinary schedule editor; this workflow never replaces it.
5. Edit a template with a reason, or set it Inactive to pause future generation. Earlier generated days and booked appointments remain unchanged. To cancel/reduce an existing day use its schedule/appointment workflow. **Refresh this template** retrieves its current revision. Dated history retains the previous patterns, officers, reasons and generation counts. Preview/print uses loaded snapshots only.

Generation is an explicit action, not a background process. No reminders, appointments, visits, patient entries, charges, receipts or stock movements are created by saving a template. There is no data migration or deletion. One atomic commit covers new daily schedules, their events, the template generation marker, a retained template event, audit and request receipt. The conditional marker prevents concurrent edits or deactivation racing generation. Stable daily IDs also prevent duplicate days from different templates or manual creation. All new collections participate in Hospital backup discovery.

Database use: a template create uses two exact reads, one clinician lookup limited to two matches and four atomic writes. Unchanged saves make zero writes and no clinician query. A preview reads its template and only the selected weekday dates (at most 31), without queries or writes. Generation uses at most 33 exact reads, one bounded clinician query and 66 atomic writes for 31 new days. Completed receipt replay uses one exact read and zero writes. A newly previewed range with no missing days writes nothing. Template lists/history use one query, fetch 31/display 30, have scope-bound cursors, and reuse the existing bounded cache. No polling, per-patient lookups or configuration-read audit writes are added. These counts exclude authentication and branch configuration.

Verification: 4,783 top-level tests passed, including 19 new recurring-clinic tests. The new browser workflow and existing appointments/waitlist browser workflows passed with synthetic records at desktop and phone widths. Coverage includes validation, stale previews, booked/manual exceptions, concurrent edits and creation, inactive clinicians, lost responses, unchanged retries, module/branch/role/subscription restrictions, dated and cached pages, no-read printing and session clearing. See `tests/hospital-recurring-clinics.test.mjs` and `scripts/verify-hospital-recurring-clinics-ui.mjs`. Live evidence is recorded in the Owner Demo guide.

## Appointment email reminders

Appointments → open a saved booking → **Preview email reminder**. This reads only the booking, its patient/contact, the matching reminder and branch email configuration. Confirm the patient's permission to use the displayed saved email, choose a send time in WAT before the appointment, then choose **Schedule reminder**. No reminders are generated merely by opening a booking or generating recurring clinics.

**Appointment reminders** holds separate Queued, Sending, Accepted, Failed, Uncertain, Cancelled and Skipped lists. Closed lists and history require a date range (up to 93 days); lists are 30 rows per page, retained while navigating. Refresh retrieves changes from other staff. Opening the appointments screen does not read reminder histories. Reception/appointment writers may schedule, cancel and retry definitely rejected messages; read-only roles can inspect them. Provider-outcome review requires appointment configuration authority.

The isolated Owner Demo scheduler checks at most five due reminders every five minutes. An authenticated endpoint verifies its Hospital workspace and current subscription/module access. Each dispatch rechecks the appointment, current contact and patient status. Changed, cancelled, elapsed or inactive bookings are skipped. The dispatch claim conditionally guards the booking and patient versions without changing their fields. The reminder then becomes Sending before any external email request. Concurrent workers and repeat clicks cannot claim it twice.

Accepted means the configured Brevo/Google provider accepted the message, not proof of inbox delivery. A definite rejection becomes Failed and needs an explicit retry after configuration is fixed. An unknown/timeout outcome becomes Uncertain, or remains Sending if the final database response was lost. Neither is automatically retried. After ten minutes an authorised officer can record evidence from the provider log and close the case as accepted or cancelled; closing an uncertain send never permits another send of that reminder. Messages contain appointment logistics only, not clinical notes or diagnoses.

For an unsent Failed/Skipped reminder or a reminder cancelled before sending, use **Preview current booking** to confirm a corrected saved email or new send time. Renew consent before scheduling. Earlier message snapshots and delivery events remain. Accepted/uncertain sends cannot use this action. A real rescheduling to a different booking slot has its own reminder identity; the old reminder remains in history.

Automatic infrastructure: `workers/hospital-appointment-reminders/wrangler.jsonc`; encrypted `HOSPITAL_REMINDER_SCHEDULER_SECRET` must match the Pages project and Worker. Deploy using an additive secrets file; never put the secret in Git. The checked-in target is only `dynamax-hospital-review`. Four additive Firestore indexes are in `firestore.hospital.indexes.json`. Patient/payment/appointment records are never deleted by this feature. There is no SMS/WhatsApp sender or automatic inbox-delivery webhook in this increment. A configured branch email provider is required for actual sends; tests use a synthetic sender and do not email patients.
