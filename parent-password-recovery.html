Parent account access

Forgot your password?

Reset the password for your parent portal account using the email address registered with the school.

Enter your email address, not the student's admission number. If you have never set a password, use the Parent Login Code supplied by the school. Contact the school if you no longer have access to your registered email.

Use your recovery code

Copy the code from your email. It expires after 15 minutes and can be used only once.

After resetting, sign in again on your devices. Your linked children, results, payments and other records stay unchanged.
