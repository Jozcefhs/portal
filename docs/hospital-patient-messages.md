# Hospital patient and guardian messages — H12

This workflow extends the secure patient portal with private, clinician-opened conversations. It is enabled only in Hospital. No clinical, financial or stock migration is required.

## Staff workflow

1. Reception verifies each patient or guardian and issues their own portal invitation under Patients and NFC cards → Patient / guardian portal access.
2. A Doctor or Medical Director opens that patient's record and selects **Open patient conversation**. Choose a named recipient, verify their authority and consent, enter a subject and first message, then start the conversation. Financial access alone does not grant conversation access.
3. Doctors, Medical Directors and Nurses with their clinical module can use **Patient messages**. Open conversations load on selecting this tab. The list indicates whose reply is awaited. Replies, closing and reopening require the current conversation version.
4. **Closed** history requires a date range of at most 93 days. Each page contains at most 30 conversations. Use Refresh to check for replies; there is no automatic polling.

Administrators, reception and finance roles cannot read clinical conversations merely because they manage portal invitations or invoices. A doctor starts each conversation; nurses can read, reply and close/reopen within their authorised branch. Subscription read-only mode prevents changes.

## Patient and guardian workflow

Sign in at `/hospital-portal.html`, then expand **Messages with your care team**. Only conversations explicitly opened for that recipient's current access in that patient/branch are returned. Open a conversation to reply. Closing it retains its messages but disables new replies; the clinical team may reopen it while that recipient's authority remains valid.

This is not an emergency service. The page directs urgent requests to the hospital. This increment sends no email or push alerts and makes no response-time promise. Staff must check their inbox. Attachments and patient-initiated conversations are not part of this increment.

## Integrity, access and resource use

- Messages are immutable, attributable records with ordered sequence numbers. Closing/reopening creates immutable status events; it does not delete messages.
- Staff writes use existing operation receipts. Portal replies use dedicated request receipts. The same request and payload replays without another write; a changed payload with that reference is rejected. Concurrent different replies use compare-and-set and cannot overwrite messages.
- Patient/guardian actions derive scope from a verified session and grant, never an admission number, card or requested patient ID. Another guardian, patient, branch or invitation generation cannot read the thread. Revocation, expiry, inactive patient and sign-out are enforced. A reissued invitation requires new clinician consent and a new conversation; old messages remain available to authorised clinical staff.
- Recipient and patient compare-and-set guards prevent replies racing with revocation or identity changes. Patient replies also guard their current session against concurrent sign-out. These checks are deliberate safety writes; list reads and conversation reads create no writes.
- Inbox, recipient and message queries fetch at most 31 documents and return 30. Cursors include a scope fingerprint. Open conversations use a bounded active queue; closed history is date-bounded. There are no collection-wide scans, polling or bulk loads.
- Browser caches retain at most 10 inbox pages in memory. Mutations invalidate them; opening a thread always revalidates authority. Logout, expiry, branch change and page exit clear sensitive content. No messages are stored in local/session storage.
- New `PortalThreads`, `PortalMessages`, `PortalMessageEvents` and `PortalMessageReceipts` collections are registered for hospital backups. Existing patients, clinical sources, invoices, payments, deposits and stock records remain intact. No charge is generated by a message.
