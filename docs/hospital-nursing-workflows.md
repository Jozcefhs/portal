# Hospital nursing charts, medication administration and handovers

Development increment: 27 October 2026. Web companion and shared backend on `codex/hospital-edition`. Test and push only; no deployment, compilation or installer release. Verification uses synthetic records.

## Staff workflow

1. Enable **Nursing and handovers** for the Hospital subscription and relevant staff. It depends on patients, reception, inpatient admissions and orders. Hospital Standard includes it by default; other editions do not gain this module. Nurses, Doctors and Medical Directors have clinical access. Administrative admission rights do not expose nursing notes.
2. Open an admitted patient through **Wards and admissions → Open nursing record**. Confirm the patient and hospital number, and review the displayed allergies. NFC patient identity remains unchanged. Opening a record reads its exact patient/admission/visit/care references, without loading history.
3. Record an **Observations**, **Fluid balance** or **Nursing note** chart. Observations support temperature, pulse, respirations, blood pressure, oxygen saturation and pain score, with explicit units. Fluid balance records intake and output in mL. Zero values remain valid. Times are entered/displayed in WAT. Entries are attributed to the signed-in officer; no clinical interpretation or treatment recommendation is generated.
4. Choose dates to load prescriptions for the admission's visit. Open a prescription and plan a dose occurrence using its instructions. Staff enter each planned time explicitly: the software does not interpret free-text frequency, calculate medication doses or create a repeating regimen automatically.
5. Open a planned dose, choose dates to load dispensed patient supply, and record **Given**, **Withheld**, **Refused**, **Unavailable** or **Omitted**. Given requires actual time, quantity in the supplied unit, the selected dispensing entry and confirmation of patient, medicine, dose, route, time and allergies. Other outcomes require a reason and consume no supply. The recorded quantity is inventory usage, not an automatically calculated clinical dose.
6. Given checks the current prescription, pharmacy review, allergy revision, batch expiry, dispensing time and remaining supplied quantity. Held/cancelled prescriptions and unreviewed allergy changes stop the operation. A pharmacist can re-review an already fully dispensed prescription after allergies change; this does not dispense or charge it again. The pharmacy return screen shows administered quantity and permits only unused supply to be returned.
7. Create a handover for the admission and shift, naming the receiving officer by staff username. Enter situation, background, nursing assessment, agreed recommendations and outstanding tasks. The recipient must be a different active clinical officer assigned to that Hospital branch. Only that recipient can acknowledge it or request clarification through **My incoming handovers**, after choosing dates. The sender can revise an unacknowledged handover with a reason; a revised handover requires a fresh response. There are no external messages or automatic task-completion claims.
8. Choose dates and a register to review charts, planned/completed doses, handovers or changes/corrections. Open an existing record for an attributed correction. Original events remain visible. The original/latest author or Medical Director may correct a record with a reason; another clinical reader cannot silently overwrite it. An acknowledged handover remains intact. Closed admissions permit historical chart/administration corrections and handover responses, but no new bedside entries.

## Integrity and concurrency

- Each mutation commits its current record, immutable event snapshot, audit and request receipt atomically. Charts use admission/type/observation-time identity; planned doses use admission/prescription/due-time identity; handovers use admission/shift identity. Both retried requests and fresh repeated submissions are protected. Changed payloads cannot reuse a completed request ID.
- Corrections append another event and update the current summary. They never erase the original observation, administration, handover or response. Chart corrections retain the original ward/bed snapshot. Medication records retain the planned location separately from the location captured at first administration entry; corrections preserve that administration location. These snapshots describe the saved context and do not infer a prior location for backdated entries.
- Medication administration updates only the patient dispensing entry's administered counter. Pharmacy stock was already deducted and charged when dispensed. Administration does not create another stock movement, charge, invoice or accounting journal. Corrections reconcile the consumed quantity, retaining original evidence. Given and pharmacy returns share conditional dispensing revisions so they cannot consume the same units concurrently.
- Given revalidates current allergies and pharmacy review. Admission/visit and care revisions serialize competing clinical updates. Nursing mutations on an active admission clear prior discharge approval and require clinician review again; earlier signed summaries remain. Transfers and discharge cannot race a nursing mutation into partial state.
- Branch, Hospital edition, module, role, patient identity and subscription write access are checked server-side. Clinical supply responses omit monetary fields. Handover recipient lookup is limited to two matching usernames, without scanning a staff directory. Administrative and finance roles do not gain chart/medication/handover access.
- Backup discovery includes `NursingCharts`, `MedicationDoses`, `NursingHandovers` and `NursingEvents`, alongside all existing records. No production records are accessed or changed in this increment.

## Reads, writes and freshness

- Workspace/tab entry loads no nursing, medication or handover records. History requires an explicit date range of at most 93 days. Branch/admission, branch/visit or branch/recipient queries fetch 31 records and display 30, with timestamp/document-ID cursor ordering for equal timestamps. There are no whole-history scans, live polling or per-row patient lookups.
- Up to 40 requested pages are retained in memory. Cached navigation causes no additional request or audit. Refresh is explicit; successful mutations clear the nursing cache. Branch/session changes clear sensitive content. Unsaved edits are protected, including after rejected saves. No records are persisted to browser storage.
- Actual clinical access requests have one access audit. Unchanged chart/administration/handover saves do not write a receipt, event or audit. A completed mutation retry reads one request receipt and writes nothing.
- Measured service-operation budgets in the synthetic tests: a new chart uses six exact document reads and one six-write commit; an ordinary Given dose uses eight exact reads and one eight-write commit. These include mutation receipt checks/writes and audit, admission/visit and patient-supply protection. A dated chart register uses four exact reads plus one bounded query; the recipient inbox uses one bounded query without patient lookups. The chosen supply page uses one bounded query and exact context references.
- These counts describe application database calls, not measured Google Cloud billing. Authentication, subscription/branch configuration, retries after conflicts and explicit UI context refreshes are separate. Index definitions are prepared in `firestore.hospital.indexes.json` and have not been deployed.
