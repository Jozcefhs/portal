# Archived memberships and empty classrooms

In the web companion, open **Academic Management → Student records → Archived memberships**.
Choose the relevant branch, school section, academic session and term at the top of Academic Management.
In desktop v3.1.52 or later, open **Academic Management → Students → Archived memberships**.
Archived duplicates are separated from the desktop's current Student Memberships register as well.

The read-only register shows each archived membership's student, admission number, previous class/arm,
retained membership's current class/arm and status, academic period, archive time (WAT) and reason.
Search by name, admission number, classroom or reason. The register does not restore or delete records.

Retained placements come from the saved membership link, not a matching name or a guessed profile field.
Later transfers are reflected in the retained placement; earlier classrooms remain visible as history.
Missing, ambiguous, cyclic or out-of-scope links are explicitly shown as unverified.

## Removing an empty classroom

Under **Classrooms → Create classrooms**, select the class, uncheck the existing arm and save.
The confirmation archives the classroom; it does not permanently delete it. The catalogue's Archive
action applies the same check. Older clients requesting Delete for a classroom are also routed through
safe archival.
On desktop, select the classroom under **Classrooms**, then choose **Remove Empty**.

Current or future student memberships in open academic periods block removal. Closed/completed periods,
archived duplicates, past transfers, scores, attendance, diaries and staff assignments do not block it.
Active legacy student profiles without a current academic membership are still checked so imported
students are not overlooked. A confirmed current membership takes precedence over a stale profile label.

Transfers reuse the existing membership and retire any duplicate current memberships. They do not create
a second active student in the destination. The previous classroom is removed from active membership
lists; original scores, attendance and movement history retain their historical context, associated
with the same student. Archiving that empty classroom does not erase or rewrite any of those records.
